<h1 align="center">gsm2sip</h1>

An Android phone and its SIM as a SIP ↔ GSM gateway for your PBX, with calls, SMS and USSD.

An independent fork of [pulpoff/gsm2sip](https://github.com/pulpoff/gsm2sip), adding Xiaomi Mi 6 digital audio support, a G.711 A-law fix, SMS/USSD through goip-bridge and SMS callbacks. Tested on Mi 6 with LineageOS 22.2, Magisk and VoLTE.
